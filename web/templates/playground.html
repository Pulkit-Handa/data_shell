Interactive playground

Build a contract. Watch the boundary enforce it.

Declare a schema and rules, upload a small CSV or JSONL, and run the real engine. You will get the same four-stage verdict, footing check, quarantining, and certificate the CLI produces — nothing here is simulated.

No file handy? Click Load the 1,000-row sample — it seeds 100 realistic defects (non-numeric amounts, null IDs, negative amounts, an unauthorized currency) and fills in the contract for you.

Contract builder
A small file (well under the 20 GiB ceiling). Uploaded to a temp dir per run.
Delivery and quarantine are isolated per tenant.

Schema — strict Tier-1 casting

Columns are cast exactly. A value that cannot be realized is refused, never coerced.

Row rules — semantic, per record

A failing record is held and quarantined. Lists are typed: ["USD","EUR"].

Batch rules — optional, whole feed

Assertions about the surviving dataset, evaluated on the whole batch.

Enforcement result

Results appear here after you run the boundary. You will see the four-stage verdict, the footing arithmetic, held/refused breakdowns, and the certificate status.
